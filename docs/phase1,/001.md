## 1.1 BACKGROUND
A Cyberdeck is not a laptop. It is a personalized modular computer built for a specific purpose—usually data recovery, network monitoring, or off-grid communication.

## 1.2 CORE COMPONENTS
1. **The Brain:** SBC (Single Board Computer).
2. **The Shell:** 3D Printed or Pelican Case.
3. **The Interface:** Mechanical Keyboard and Display.

## 1.3 EXERCISE
* Define your primary mission for your build.
* Research the "Grid-Down" scenario.
